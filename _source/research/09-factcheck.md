# Fact-check: "Running Persistent AI Worlds" (Feb 2026 edition)

The paper needs corrections before the new edition. Of the 25 claims, 4 are wrong in whole or part (#15, #16, #21, #22), 2 are out of date (#13, #23), 7 are imprecise (#4, #10, #11, #12, #18, #24, #25) and 12 check out.

**How this was checked:** the session's web search limit ran out partway through, so everything after that came from opening the primary pages directly (arXiv, lab blogs, GitHub, conference proceedings). A few sub-claims were never checked and are labelled "not checked" below. Nothing was passed without a source.

## Most serious errors

1. **#22 OASIS:** the "10,000+ agents for polarisation" threshold is not in the paper. Polarisation appeared with 196 users; the 100-versus-10,000 comparison is about the herd effect.
2. **#16 PolyWorld:** the 1994 primary source says the opposite of "evolved signalling".
3. **#21:** "85% accuracy" misreads a figure measured relative to people's own two-week consistency. The paper has since been retitled with new numbers, and the funding line is out of date.
4. **#12, #23, #24 JaxMARL:** they repeat a speed-up wording the authors withdrew in Nov 2024. The "consumer GPU" detail can't be traced, and the install line in #24 gives CPU-only JAX, which contradicts both speed claims.
5. **#15:** the survival-reward claim is unsupported. PolyWorld actually starts with a fitness function, and "most open-ended" conflicts with #3.
6. **#4:** 132.3 million episodes is the cost of reaching stage 4, not the whole run, and stage 1 is mislabelled "random".
7. **#18:** ASAL is overstated; its abstract says "like", not "more than".
8. **#10 and #11:** the Lenski stepping-stone finding is overstated, and Flow-Lenia is dated 2025 when it was introduced in 2022.

## Internal consistency (separate from whether claims match sources)

- #3 and #15 pull against each other: one says no system is unboundedly open-ended, the other credits two systems with "the most open-ended outcomes".
- #12 and #23 repeat the same stale figure, and #24 contradicts both.
- #18 and #25 credit ASAL differently ("Sakana AI" versus "MIT/Sakana"), and both are incomplete.
- #13's star count was a snapshot and has gone stale.

The US$100M figure is taken from Index Ventures' own page, not from simile.com.

Primary PDFs read: hide-and-seek, Capture the Flag, Tierra, Lenski 2003, Moreno 2024 and PolyWorld.
