## 5. World models: when the simulator itself is learned

The first edition barely mentioned world models. They are now the fastest-moving area in this paper.

### 5.1 Two lines that have joined

From 2018 to 2023 a world model lived *inside* an agent. Ha and Schmidhuber trained a controller entirely inside its own learned "dream" of a VizDoom game and transferred the policy back to the real game [@ha2018]. MuZero planned with a learned model and matched AlphaZero without being told the rules [@schrittwieser2020]. **DreamerV3** learned behaviours by imagining their outcomes, solved more than 150 tasks with one configuration, and became the first algorithm to collect diamonds in Minecraft from scratch with no human data (published in Nature in 2025) [@hafner2025nature].

From 2024 the world model became the *environment*. Google DeepMind's **Genie** (February 2024) learned a playable 2D world from 30,000 hours of unlabelled internet video [@bruce2024]; **GameNGen** ran the game DOOM at 20 frames per second as a neural network, with no game engine, holding a little over three seconds of history [@valevski2024]; **Oasis** (Decart and Etched, October 2024) did the same for a Minecraft-like world [@oasis2024]; and Microsoft's **Muse** (Nature, February 2025) generated gameplay that stayed consistent for up to two minutes [@muse2025].

In late 2025 and 2026 the two lines joined: agents began to *learn inside* generated worlds.

### 5.2 Genie 3, SIMA 2 and Dreamer 4

**Genie 3** (August 2025) generates interactive 3D worlds from a text prompt at 720p and 24 frames per second. Worlds stay consistent for "a few minutes of continuous interaction, rather than extended hours", with a visual memory of about one minute, and users can trigger "promptable world events" such as a change of weather [@genie3]. It remains the best general-purpose model in October 2026: it ranked first on WorldRoamBench's first-person test, an independent benchmark of twelve models published in June 2026 [@xu2026worldroam]. No successor had been announced by 10 October 2026.

**SIMA 2** (November 2025) is a Gemini-based agent that follows instructions in commercial 3D games, roughly doubles the success rate of the original SIMA, and improves itself through self-directed play, including in worlds generated by Genie 3 [@sima2]. **Dreamer 4** (September 2025) learned to collect diamonds in Minecraft purely offline, from 2,541 hours of recorded play and no environment interaction, by training inside a world model of about two billion parameters that runs in real time on one GPU [@hafner2025dreamer4]. The result is a milestone, and its numbers are sobering: diamonds in 0.7% of episodes, an iron pickaxe in 29%.

**Public access** arrived as a demonstration, not a simulator. *Project Genie* (29 January 2026) put Genie 3 in a web app for US subscribers to Google's top AI plan, with each generation capped at 60 seconds; in May 2026 it added worlds grounded in Google Street View and began rolling out more widely [@projectgenie2026]. Waymo built its driving simulator on Genie 3 [@waymo2026].

Three facts about this landscape matter for anyone who wants to run a persistent world:

1. **Consistency lasts minutes, not hours.** Every horizon confirmed from a primary source is a few minutes at most. WorldRoamBench measured memory scores falling from 65.4 to 41.3 between short and long rollouts, even within tests of 10 to 60 seconds [@xu2026worldroam]. World Labs estimates that an hour of persistence by brute force would need contexts of over 100 million tokens, which it calls "neither feasible nor economically viable" [@rtfm2025].
2. **Physics is the weakest part.** On WorldRoamBench every open model scored below 8 out of 100 on mechanics, against Genie 3's 61.5 [@xu2026worldroam]; on Meta's IntPhys 2 benchmark video models perform at or near chance while humans score 85% to 95% [@assran2025].
3. **The open models need datacentre hardware.** The interactive models confirmed to run on one consumer GPU are small or old (DIAMOND at 10 fps on an RTX 3090 [@alonso2024]); the strong open ones need an H100 or a rack (Matrix-Game 3.0 used nine GPUs).

> **Key insight:** In October 2026 no world model can be the authoritative record of a long-running simulation. Keep the state of your world in an explicit store or game engine, and use world models for what they are good at: rendering, generating assets (Marble, HY-World 2.0) and short-range imagination for planning, in the style of Dreamer.
