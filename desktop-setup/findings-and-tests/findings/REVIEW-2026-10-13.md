## Errors inside Section 11

1. **11.1, the cost of running two conditions at once.** "Two conditions run together finish 14% sooner
   than the same two run one after the other, at the cost of each individual seed taking 43% longer."
   A 1.139 throughput gain saves 1 − 17.688 / 20.153 = **12%** of the time, not 14%. And each process
   runs at 0.630 against 1.095 years/s: **43% slower**, which means each seed takes 1.095 / 0.630 =
   **74% longer**, not 43%. Suggested: "finish about 12% sooner ..., at the cost of each seed running
   43% slower (taking 74% longer)". "Most of a day" over a week still holds (12% of 7 days is 0.85 days).
2. **The prediction count.** Section 11's opening and 11.8 both say "twenty-seven" written before the
   first world. `predictions.md` holds 29 numbered items before its first correction line: T1 and 28 across
   E1 to E6. Three are falsifiers folded into the predictions they guard (E1.4, E2.5, E5.5), which leaves
   the **26** that Table 15 scores and FINDINGS.md counts. Suggested: "twenty-nine numbered items, three of
   them falsifiers scored with the predictions they guard: twenty-six in all".
3. **11.4 against Table 13, land cells.** 11.4 says "the world's 8,494 land cells"; Table 13 says 8,520
   for `base`. Terrain differs by seed, so one is presumably a single seed and the other a mean. Use 8,520
   in both, or say which seed 8,494 is.
4. **11.2, "about where the blank-slate world's was at year 300".** Copy at year 2,000 is 0.057; none
   was 0.080 at year 100 and 0.042 at year 300. Suggested: "between where the blank-slate world stood at
   years 100 and 300".
5. **11.7 against 11.2, the ratio at year 1,000.** 11.7 gives 2.6 and the 11.2 table gives 2.5
   (0.084 / 0.034 = 2.47). As the findings README says, the tables average differently. Suggested: one
   line under the 11.2 year-by-year table ("values in the stated year") and under Tables 9 and 14 ("mean
   of the last 100 years"), then quote 2.5 in 11.7 to match the table it cites, or say it is a 100-year mean.
